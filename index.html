AI-HUMAN ALIGNMENT AND COOPERATION

Long-term memory
that learns how to cooperate.

AICO is an open research framework for AI that does more than retain history. It learns who it is working with, what a relationship means now, and how a conversation should move forward.

Not a chat archive. A living model for collaboration.

AICO treats long-term interaction as an evolving alignment process, not a longer context window.

01 / THE THESIS

Memory should change
what AI does next.

Most systems remember facts, retrieve history, or inject a profile into context. AICO asks a harder question: can long-term information shape an AI's judgment, strategy, and conduct in the present moment?

For AICO, continuity is not only factual. It is behavioral: a response should reflect relationship stage, topic, purpose, pace, and the authority of the people involved.

MEMORY AS STORAGE

What happened before?

Facts, preferences, transcripts, and retrieved passages are placed back into context.

MEMORY AS ALIGNMENT

What is appropriate now?

Personal history, relationship context, topic, and strategy inform the next cooperative action.

02 / A MEMORY MODEL WITH STRUCTURE

Remember the world
around the person.

AICO does not indiscriminately inject all history. It constructs local, relevant context for the relationship and purpose of the present interaction.

03 / FROM ALIGNMENT TO COOPERATION

A response is a move
in a longer relationship.

AICO organizes more than retrieval. It uses a dialogue strategy tree to turn context into an intentional multi-turn path: understand, establish common ground, enter the core request, and adjust from real feedback.

04 / AUTHORITY AND CONFIRMATION

Alignment is not
AI deciding alone.

AICO separates proposal from authority. The model may surface a candidate topic, profile update, relationship interpretation, or strategy branch. The system preserves evidence and governance; people retain meaningful control.

PPERSONAL

Calibrated with the person

The user can confirm, revise, reject, or revoke a candidate understanding of their own preferences, relationships, and strategies.

Authority: user confirmation
EEXPERT

Grounded in professional judgment

AI may suggest candidate branches, but the expert retains final authority over professional logic, service style, and decision rules.

Authority: expert confirmation
