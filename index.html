AI-HUMAN ALIGNMENT AND COOPERATION

Long-term memory
that learns how to cooperate.

AICO is an open research framework for AI that does more than retain history. It learns who it is working with, what a relationship means now, and how a conversation should move forward.

01 / THE PREMISE

"A thousand readers, a thousand Hamlets."
An interpretive maxim inspired by Shakespeare's Hamlet

Long-term memory
gives AI continuity.

Personal alignment gives that continuity meaning. AICO begins from the premise that the same words, memories, and goals can mean different things across people, relationships, and moments.

AICO does not assume one universally correct way for AI to interact. It distinguishes shared facts and constraints from the personal, relational question of how AI can cooperate appropriately with people now.

SHARED GROUND

What remains true for everyone?

Facts, safety boundaries, explicit commitments, and professional constraints remain accountable and open to verification.

PERSONAL ALIGNMENT

What is appropriate here?

Personal memory, relationship context, dialogue purpose, and feedback inform the next cooperative action.

02 / RESEARCH FOCUS

Four research questions
for lasting cooperation.

AICO treats long-term interaction as a structured research problem, not a longer context window. Its work is organized around memory, relationships, strategy, and feedback-grounded cooperation with people.

01

02

Relationship-aware AI

03

Strategy-guided dialogue

How can a dialogue strategy tree organize multi-turn cooperation, feedback, and change?

04

Feedback-grounded alignment

How should different feedback sources shape alignment in PERSONAL and EXPERT modes?

05 / FEEDBACK-GROUNDED ALIGNMENT

Different feedback,
different alignment rules.

AICO learns cooperation from feedback, but does not treat every signal alike. PERSONAL integrates feedback from the user, interaction partners, the system, and model evaluation; EXPERT requires the expert's confirmation before professional logic is made persistent.

PPERSONAL

Learns from everyday feedback

The user is the long-term aligned subject. AI can propose and reversibly update personal, relationship, topic, and strategy understanding from user corrections, partner feedback, system observations, and model evaluation.

User confirmation is a strong signal
EEXPERT

Expert confirmation is required

The expert is the long-term aligned subject; the client supplies service context. AI may propose a small candidate extension, but only expert-confirmed logic becomes persistent.

Expert confirmation is required
